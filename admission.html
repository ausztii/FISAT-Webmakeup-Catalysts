B.Tech

Six engineering programs: CSE, ECE, EEE, EIE, Mechanical and Civil. All NBA accredited.

01

Appear for KEAM

A KEAM rank is compulsory for B.Tech admission at FISAT, whether you choose the government or the management quota.

02

Pick your quota

Government quota seats are allotted by the CEE Kerala office. For management quota, register through the FISAT website.

03

Rank list

For Computer Science and Electronics & Communication under management quota, the rank list is based on your KEAM score, not Plus Two marks.

04

Report with documents

Bring your documents and pay the fees at the admission desk. Use the checklist below to get ready.

MBA

Two years. Full time. FISAT Business School, batch 2026–28.

01

Apply

FISAT Business School invites applications for its two-year full-time MBA program, Batch 2026–28.

02

Admission test

Your score in the admission test is the first part of the selection.

03

GD & interview

Group discussion and a personal interview complete the process.

04

Choose a specialisation

Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, Information Systems, Production & Operations, and International Business.

MCA

Two-year program running at FISAT since 2006.

01

Check eligibility

The two-year MCA is open to graduates such as B.Tech, B.Sc and B.Com. Confirm the exact criteria on the official page.

02

Pick your route

Admission is through the government or management quota. A separate application and fees are remitted at the time of admission.

03

Prepare documents

Admit card and undertaking forms are available from the admission page.

04

Also on offer

FISAT also runs an Integrated MCA. Ask the MCA desk for details.
